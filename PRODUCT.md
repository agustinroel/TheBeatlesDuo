# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Angular 22 (Standalone Components, Signals, native control flow), GSAP (core + ScrollTrigger), custom SCSS/CSS, static deployment on Vercel.

## Users
Fans of live music and The Beatles across the Levante/Costa Blanca region (Alicante, Torrevieja, Moraira, Murcia) looking for upcoming acoustic/tribute performances, as well as venue owners, pub managers, and event organizers seeking to book "The Beatles Duo".

## Product Purpose
Provide a fast, mobile-first single-page experience showcasing "The Beatles Duo", their complete 2027 tour schedule across venues, and a direct, frictionless WhatsApp booking channel.

## Positioning
An intimate, high-craft acoustic and vocal tribute duo channeling the raw, analog spirit of Liverpool's Cavern Club into live performances across iconic local pubs and clubs.

## Operating Context
Visitors primarily access the site on mobile devices via social media links, QR codes at venues, or word-of-mouth while checking tonight's or this month's live music schedule, or when contacting the duo directly via WhatsApp for bookings.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static SPA with zero backend dependencies, built for instant loading on Vercel.
- Mobile-first ergonomics with generous touch targets, zero horizontal overflow, and fluid `clamp()` typography.
- Direct booking action linked to `https://wa.me/34663034905`.
- Semantic placeholder container in the Hero section prepared for a future custom illustration of the two musicians (currently housing a minimalist bespoke SVG guitar pick/string motif).

## Brand Commitments
- **Name:** The Beatles Duo
- **Aesthetic Direction:** "Cavern Club" — charcoal black (`#111111`) ground with subtle analog CSS noise/grain, warm cream/off-white primary text, vintage brick red / aged gold accents reserved for interactions and CTAs, high-contrast editorial serif display (`Playfair Display`) paired with geometric sans-serif (`Montserrat` / `Inter`) for schedule data and controls.

Confirmed 2027 live performance schedule:
- **Paddy's Point (Torrevieja):** 4 Jan, 15 Feb, 22 Mar, 12 Apr, 10 May, 14 Jun, 12 Jul, 9 Aug, 6 Sep, 11 Oct, 8 Nov 2027.
- **Saxo (Moraira):** First Sunday of each month from Jan to Oct 2027 (3 Jan, 7 Feb, 7 Mar, 4 Apr, 2 May, 6 Jun, 4 Jul, 1 Aug, 5 Sep, 3 Oct 2027), plus Friday 5 Nov 2027 (18:00 hs) and Saturday 4 Dec 2027.

- **Direct Booking Contact:** `https://wa.me/34663034905`

## Product Principles
- **Mobile-Native Precision:** Every row, filter, and CTA is engineered for thumb reach and smooth 60fps scroll choreography on mobile viewports.
- **Analog Intimacy Over Digital Clutter:** Strip away unnecessary chrome so the typography, schedule hierarchy, and analog grain carry the atmosphere.
- **Immediate Actionability:** Dates are scannable in seconds and booking takes a single tap to WhatsApp.
